## About Me

I'm an **Artificial Intelligence & Data Engineering student at Istanbul Technical University (ITU)** and a **Software Team Member at ITUNOM UAV Team**.

I develop software for autonomous UAV systems, working across mission logic, multi-UAV coordination, real-time telemetry, MAVLink communication, and computer vision. Through ITUNOM, this work includes autonomous systems in the context of **TEKNOFEST Savaşan İHA**.

Before focusing on AI and autonomous systems, I built production full-stack applications. That experience shapes how I approach engineering today: understanding individual components, connecting them into complete systems, and considering how those systems behave in operation.

My broader interests are **autonomous systems, robotics, computer vision, and applied AI**.

## Current Focus

- **Autonomous Systems**  
  Mission logic, swarm coordination, and communication for autonomous multi-UAV systems.

- **Computer Vision**  
  Object detection, tracking, and perception for UAV autonomy.

- **AI & Data Engineering**  
  Developing my foundations in AI and data engineering, with an interest in their application to intelligent systems.

- **Software Engineering**  
  Connecting telemetry, shared system state, and flight-system interfaces into reliable software.
